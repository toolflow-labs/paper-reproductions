# Reproduction notes

## Official implementation check

As of 2026-09-20, searches by exact title, DOI `10.1016/j.ijepes.2025.111120`, first author Shiwei Xia, and CVaR/ADN keywords did not locate an official public GitHub repository or public dataset. The paper states `Data will be made available on request` and reports a MATLAB/YALMIP/GUROBI implementation.

## What is retained from the paper

- IEEE 33-bus benchmark, 12.66 kV
- total benchmark load around 3.715 MW + j2.3 MVAr
- WT at buses 17/32, 0.9 MW each
- PV at buses 21/24, 0.6 MW each
- ESS at bus 15: 1.8 MWh / 0.3 MW
- ESS at bus 32: 1.0 MWh / 0.2 MW
- 24 hourly periods
- 1000 uncertainty scenarios reduced to 20 representatives
- time-of-use tariff
- Case 2 vs Case 3 risk/mean trade-off
- CVaR as a tail-risk term

## Major upgrade: pandapower AC power flow

The earlier reproduction used a hand-written LinDistFlow approximation. The current version replaces that layer with:

1. `pandapower.networks.case33bw()`;
2. PV/WT as `sgen` elements;
3. ESS as `storage` elements;
4. `pandapower.runpp(..., algorithm="bfsw")` for each scenario-hour;
5. finite-difference AC sensitivities around each scenario-hour operating point;
6. full AC power-flow validation after the ESS schedule is selected.

Pandapower uses the consumer sign convention for storage: **positive p_mw means charging and negative p_mw means discharging**.

## Why the optimizer is still outside pandapower

The paper's dispatch problem is multi-period and multi-scenario:

- 20 uncertainty scenarios
- 24 time steps
- SOC dynamics linking time steps
- common ESS sequence
- CVaR auxiliary variables

This is not represented by one standard single-period pandapower `runopp()` call. The reproduction therefore uses SciPy/HiGHS for the coupled schedule optimization, with network coefficients calibrated from pandapower AC power flow. Final results are scored again with full AC power flow.

## Remaining substitutions

- Fig. 8 load/PV/WT series are reconstructed because the raw values are unavailable.
- The exact TSL forecast-error parameters are unavailable; Student-t/Normal error models are transparent substitutes.
- Gaussian-copula spatiotemporal dependence substitutes for the paper's exact PED implementation.
- Full OLTC/CB/SOP/SVC/DR/network-reconfiguration co-optimization is not reproduced.
- The paper's exact numerical beta was not identified in the available text; this reproduction uses beta=0.95.
- Some cost/risk scaling parameters remain reproduction assumptions.

## Interpretation boundary

This is still a method-level clean-room reproduction, not a claim of exact Table 4-6 numerical replication. The important improvement is that distribution-network voltages, slack-bus import and active line losses are now generated by a standard AC power-flow engine rather than a hand-written LinDistFlow approximation.
