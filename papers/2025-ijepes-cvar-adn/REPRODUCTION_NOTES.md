# Reproduction notes

## Official implementation check

As of 2026-09-20, searches by exact title, DOI `10.1016/j.ijepes.2025.111120`, first author Shiwei Xia, and CVaR/ADN keywords did not locate an official public GitHub repository or public dataset. The paper states `Data will be made available on request` and reports a MATLAB/YALMIP/GUROBI implementation.

## Kept from the paper

- IEEE 33-bus base system, 12.66 kV, 3.715 MW + j2.3 MVAr
- WT at buses 17/32, 0.9 MW each
- PV at buses 21/24, 0.6 MW each
- ESS at bus 15: 1.8 MWh / 0.3 MW; bus 32: 1.0 MWh / 0.2 MW
- 24 hourly periods
- 1000 uncertainty scenarios reduced to 20 representative scenarios
- paper time-of-use prices
- CVaR tail-risk objective and Case-2 vs Case-3 trade-off logic
- voltage reference band 0.94–1.06 p.u.

## Transparent substitutions

- Fig. 8 curves are approximated from the published trend because raw series are unavailable.
- TSL parameters are not published; Student-t(df=5) with stated scale assumptions is used.
- Spatiotemporal dependence is generated by a Gaussian copula instead of the paper's PED reordering.
- LinDistFlow replaces the paper's MISOCP/second-order-cone DistFlow.
- Network reconfiguration, OLTC, CB, SOP, SVC, and DR are omitted so the reproduction stays solver-independent and focuses on the CVaR/ESS mechanism.
- The paper does not expose a numerical beta in the available text; beta=0.95 is used.
- Network-risk penalty coefficient is a reproduction tuning parameter, not a paper value.

## Interpretation

The reproduction is intended to reproduce the qualitative experimental finding: adding a CVaR tail-risk term changes the storage schedule and can reduce extreme voltage-risk losses at a modest economic premium. It is not a claim of exact Table 4/Table 5 numerical replication.
